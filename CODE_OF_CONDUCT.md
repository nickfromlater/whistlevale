# Be a good neighbor

Welcome newcomers, credit other people's work and give specific, respectful
feedback. People of all backgrounds and experience levels should feel able to
contribute. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, spam and disclosure of
private information are not welcome.

Keep discussions about the work. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content,
close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. For a sensitive concern,
ask the maintainer for a private contact route without posting anyone's personal
information in an issue. Treat reports with care and share only what is needed
to address them.
